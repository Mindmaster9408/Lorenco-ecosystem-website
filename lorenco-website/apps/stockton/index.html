Stockton

Know what you have. Know where it is.

Stockton keeps your stock organised across warehouses, from purchase order to sale, so the till and the stock room see the same numbers.

Book a demoOpen Stockton
Features

What Stockton does

Stock items

Every item, with its costs and quantities.

Multiple warehouses

Keep stock in more than one place and see each one separately.

Stock movements

Every movement recorded, so you can always trace what happened.

Suppliers and purchase orders

Order from suppliers and book stock in against the order.

Costing and valuation

Stock valued properly, so your books reflect reality.

Work orders

Basic manufacturing: turn raw materials into finished goods.

Works with Checkout Charlie

Sales at the till update stock. No double capturing.
