Till, stock, books and payroll.
One place.

Stop paying for five separate subscriptions and re-typing the same numbers between them. Capture it once, and it flows.

Till→Stock→Books←Payroll
A day in your business

Each app picks up where the last one left off.

At the counter

Checkout Charlie rings up the sale and cashes up the till.

In the stock room

Stockton sees the sale and updates what's on the shelf and what it's worth.

In the books

Ledger Leo gets the sales and the stock value, reconciles the bank and prepares VAT.

On pay day

Paytime runs payroll and posts it to the ledger as a journal.

Why it matters

Fewer mistakes. Less double work.

Your data isn't stuck on one PC

It lives in one secure central database. Change a laptop or a till and lose nothing.

More than one company?

Run each one fully separate and switch between them in one click.

Your accountant can log in too

Give your accountant their own access, so the books stay up to date without emailing spreadsheets.
