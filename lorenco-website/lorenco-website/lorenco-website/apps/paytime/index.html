Payroll
Lorenco Paytime

South African payroll, done properly.

Lorenco Paytime works out PAYE, UIF and SDL for you, handles overtime and bonuses correctly, and locks every pay run once it's final.

Book a demoOpen Lorenco Paytime
Features

What Lorenco Paytime does

Pay runs and payslips

Run payroll and hand out clear payslips.

PAYE, UIF and SDL

Worked out automatically on every pay run.

Overtime and short time

Capture hours and Paytime does the maths.

Once-off bonuses taxed correctly

Bonuses get the right tax treatment, not a guess.

Attendance and leave

Keep track of who was in and who was on leave.

Payroll items with IRP5 codes

Every earning and deduction mapped to the right IRP5 code.

PAYE reconciliation

Reconcile what you deducted with what you declared.

Historical payroll import

Moving from another system? Bring your payroll history with you.

Locked, final pay runs

Once a pay run is finalised it can't be changed. A clean audit trail, every month.
