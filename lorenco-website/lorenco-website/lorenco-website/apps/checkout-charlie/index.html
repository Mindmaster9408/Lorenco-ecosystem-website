Point of sale
Checkout Charlie

The till that keeps up with your counter.

Checkout Charlie is a fast, friendly point of sale for retail counters. Scan, sell, cash up and go home knowing the numbers add up.

Book a demoOpen Checkout Charlie
Features

What Checkout Charlie does

Barcode scanning

Scan products straight onto the sale. Quick at the counter, accurate on the stock sheet.

Tills and cash-ups

Open and close tills, cash up per shift and see exactly where the money went.

Customers and account sales

Sell on account to regular customers and give them their own discounts.

Manager-PIN approvals

Discounts and returns need a manager's PIN, so nothing slips through.

Cash paid-out tracking

Record cash taken out of the till, with a reason, so cash-ups still balance.

Receipts

Clean receipts for every sale.

Serial-number tracking

Track high-value items by serial number from receiving to sale.

Receiving-clerk role

Staff who only book in deliveries get a role that does just that.

Other currencies ready

Set up for other currencies and countries, including the UK, when you need it.
