Security

Your numbers are your business.
We keep them that way.

Here's how the Lorenco Ecosystem keeps your data safe, separate and properly controlled.

Your data in the cloud

All business data lives in one secure central database, not on a single PC or till. Lose a laptop, keep your data.

Every company kept separate

Each company's data is kept apart from every other company's. Practices see only the clients they look after.

Access by role

People see only what their job needs. A cashier doesn't see payroll.

Locked payroll

Finalised pay runs can't be changed, so payroll history stays exactly as it was paid.

Audit trails

Important actions are recorded, so you can see who did what.

Approvals built in

Manager PINs for discounts and returns, so nothing changes without the right person saying yes.

The right access for each person.

Questions

Straight answers.

The things business owners and accountants ask us most. Something else on your mind? Ask us.

Where is my data kept?

In one secure central cloud database, not on a single PC or till. If a laptop breaks or a till is replaced, your data is still there.

Can other businesses see my data?

No. Every company's data is kept separate. An accounting practice only sees the clients it looks after.

Who in my business can see what?

Each person gets a role, such as owner, accountant, payroll admin, store manager or cashier, and only sees what that role needs.

Can someone change last month's payroll?

No. Once a pay run is finalised it's locked, so it stays exactly as it was paid.
