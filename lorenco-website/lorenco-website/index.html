Questions

Straight answers.

The things business owners and accountants ask us most. Something else on your mind? Ask us.

Do I have to use all the apps?

No. Start with one app, like Paytime or Checkout Charlie, and add more when you're ready. Everything you add uses the same login and the same data.

Is it difficult to move over from my current system?

Not as hard as you'd think. Paytime can import your historical payroll, and Ledger Leo can import bank statements, including PDF statements from the major SA banks. In a demo we'll look at what you use today and how you'd move across.

Can I run more than one company?

Yes. Each company is kept fully separate, and you switch between them in one click. Accounting practices use the same feature to look after many clients.

Does it connect to the other tools I use?

Tell us what you use and we'll tell you honestly what connects today. Inside the ecosystem, the till, stock, books and payroll already work together.

Is it only for South African businesses?

It's built for South African compliance: PAYE, UIF, SDL, IRP5 codes and VAT. Checkout Charlie and Stockton also support other currencies and countries, including the UK.
